# Conversion blueprint

## Home sequence

1. Announcement bar.
2. Header and visual mega-menu.
3. Campaign hero carousel.
4. Shop by category.
5. New & Popular.
6. Promotional or bundle banner.
7. RELIVANOW ecosystem explanation.
8. Social gallery / UGC.
9. Press or testimonials.
10. Brand manifesto.
11. Newsletter.
12. Footer.

Every section must discover, compare, resolve an objection, prove trust, or enable purchase. Decorative sections without a funnel role are excluded.

## PDP purchase sequence

1. Rating.
2. Product name.
3. Tagline.
4. Price and real saving.
5. Shipping, returns, warranty, and support.
6. Financing when available in the market.
7. Key benefits.
8. Style variant.
9. Color variant.
10. Delivery estimate.
11. Compatible add-ons.
12. Bundle.
13. Quantity.
14. Add to Cart.
15. Payment and security reassurance.

Desktop uses gallery left and sticky purchase column right. Mobile uses a tactile media carousel and vertical purchase flow.

## Internal navigation

- PURCHASE.
- OVERVIEW.
- SPECS.
- FAQ.
- REVIEWS.

## PDP storytelling sequence

1. Emotional outcome.
2. Camera and monitoring.
3. App, schedules, and portions.
4. Freshness, reliability, and contingencies.
5. Cleaning and food compatibility.
6. App/ecosystem.
7. Installation video.
8. Accessible specifications.
9. FAQ.
10. Reviews.
11. Related products.

## Cart drawer

- Correct product/variant and line properties.
- Reliable AJAX quantity/remove.
- Real free-shipping progress.
- Total savings.
- Maximum three relevant cross-sells.
- Discount field only with a reliable supported flow.
- One dominant black checkout CTA.
- No fake timers and no involuntary PDP redirects.

## AOV order

1. Main product.
2. Single-to-Dual upgrade.
3. Compatible accessory.
4. Feeder + fountain bundle.
5. Compatible replacement consumables.
6. Cart cross-sell.
