# RELIVANOW project brief

## Objective

Build a premium, conversion-focused Shopify store for RELIVANOW Smart Pet Care using Horizon 4.1.1. Recreate the hierarchy, commercial logic, responsive quality, and product storytelling associated with PETLIBRO while using RELIVANOW branding, copy, media, product facts, reviews, and assets.

## Initial product

**Working name:** RELIVANOW Smart Automatic Pet Feeder.

Working commercial structure, pending validation:

- Single Bowl: USD 129.
- Dual Bowl: USD 159.
- Colors: Cloud White, Graphite, and Sky Blue when physically available.

These values are not permission to create Shopify data or publish an offer. `docs/CONTENT_MATRIX.md` governs approval.

## Audience

Pet owners who value reliable routines, remote visibility, simple technology, and reassurance when away from home. The brand should feel like a specialist pet-tech company, not a generic dropshipping store.

## Value proposition

Smart pet-care products that make feeding, hydration, and daily monitoring more predictable, effortless, and connected.

## Funnel priorities

1. Reliable product discovery.
2. PDP purchase confidence.
3. Variant and cart reliability.
4. AOV through relevant upgrades, add-ons, and bundles.
5. LTV through compatible consumables and ecosystem products.

## V1 scope

- Global shell: announcement, header, visual mega-menu, predictive search, footer, newsletter.
- Conversion-focused smart-feeder PDP.
- Reliable AJAX cart drawer.
- Add-ons and bundles after commercial/technical approval.
- Home structured around categories, featured products, proof, and brand story.
- Accessible specs, FAQ, reviews, analytics, SEO, and responsive behavior.

## Non-goals

- Copying PETLIBRO code, text, media, reviews, brand, packaging, or claims.
- Direct HTML-to-Liquid conversion of the reference site.
- Reproducing PETLIBRO's third-party script and app debt.
- Adding unsupported checkout customizations.

## Success indicators

- PDP Add-to-Cart rate target: 5–10% initial range.
- Checkout initiated / ATC: above 45% initial target.
- Purchase CVR: 2–4% depending on market and traffic quality.
- AOV lift: 10–20% from relevant add-ons/bundles.
- Cart upsell take rate: 5–12% initial target.
- LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, INP < 200ms as implementation objectives.
